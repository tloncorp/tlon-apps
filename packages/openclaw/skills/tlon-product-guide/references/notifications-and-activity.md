# Notifications and activity

Finding out about new messages and controlling how much the app tells you: the Activity tab, unread numbers and dots, notification levels for the whole app or one chat, muting, push notifications on the phone, and browser and desktop alerts.

## See what's new on the Activity tab

Phone: tap the bell in the tab bar. The screen is headed `Activity` and has three filters at the top: `All`, `Mentions` (posts that mention you) and `Replies`. Pull down to refresh.
Desktop: click the bell in the left rail. The list fills the sidebar column.
Notes: it lists mentions, replies to you or in threads you're in, new messages in chats that notify you, reactions to your posts, join requests, flagged posts, notes added or edited, and contacts' profile updates. `All` and `Replies` only show what your notification levels let through (`All` also shows profile updates), so a silenced chat stays out. The exception is a mention: one the app receives live is listed under `Mentions` even from a chat set to `Nothing`, until you pull down to refresh or reopen the app.

## Open a message or request from the Activity tab

Phone: tap the row. A message opens its chat at that message. A reply opens the thread. A reaction opens the post that was reacted to. A request to join opens the group's member list. A contact's profile update opens their profile. A note opens the notebook at that note.
Desktop: chats and threads open in the Home or Messages view you used last. Member lists and profiles open beside the Activity list.
Notes: each chat or thread gets one row showing its latest message, with text like "+2 more" when more are unread. Opening a join request also marks that group as read.

## Tell which Activity items are new

Phone: rows that arrived since you last looked at Activity have a tinted background. A dot in front of a row's title means that chat, thread or group still has something unread. A dot on the bell in the tab bar means something new and unread has arrived that you're set to be notified about.
Desktop: the bell in the left rail gets the same dot.
Notes: the tint and the bell's dot clear about a second after you open Activity. A row's own dot clears only when you read that chat or thread; looking at Activity doesn't mark messages as read. With a Bot tab, new messages from your bot light the Bot tab instead of the bell.

## Mark everything as read

Phone: on the Activity tab, tap the three-dot icon at the top right, tap `Mark all as read`, then confirm with `Mark all read`.
Desktop: the same three-dot icon sits at the top of the Activity list.
Notes: this clears the unread numbers on every chat and every notification, not only the Activity list. On the phone it also clears the number on the app icon. There is no undo. To clear one chat only, press and hold it on the Workspaces tab and tap `Mark as read`, or `Mark all as read` for a group.

## Activity is empty

Phone: a new account with nothing to show says `There is no activity... yet.` with two buttons: `Invite Friends`, which opens your personal invite sheet, and `View Contacts`.
Desktop: only `Invite Friends` is shown.
Notes: if there is activity but the filter you picked has none, the list just says `No activity`. Switch back to `All`.

## What the unread numbers and dots mean

Phone: on the Workspaces tab, a chat with unread messages shows a number in a pill at the right of its row. Channels in a group's channel list show one too. The pill is coloured when something in there is set to notify you and grey when it isn't. Above 256 it reads "256+". A muted chat shows a small crossed-out bell beside the number. Newer versions of the app keep the bell there when nothing is unread.
Desktop: the same numbers sit in the sidebar. Hovering a row swaps the number for the three-dot menu button.
Notes: in the tab bar only the bell and the Bot tab ever show a dot; the Workspaces tab doesn't. On desktop only the bell does, never the Home or Messages icons.

## Find where the new messages start in a chat

Phone: open the chat. A coloured pill sits above the first unread message and reads something like "3 new messages below", sometimes with the date in front.
Desktop: the same pill appears. A chat only counts as read while you're active in the window; after about a minute without moving the mouse or typing, an open chat stops being marked read.
Notes: opening a chat marks it read once it has loaded, which clears its number on the list.

## Choose what the whole app notifies you about

Phone: on the Settings tab, under `App`, tap `Notifications` and pick one of three levels.
Desktop: click the gear near the bottom of the left rail, then `Notifications`.
Notes: `All DMs and group posts` is the default: every DM, and every post, mention and reply in your groups. `DMs, mentions, and replies only` keeps DMs but limits groups to messages that mention you or reply to your posts. `Nothing` turns everything off, even with push allowed on the phone. The choice covers push notifications and what shows in Activity, is saved on your node so it follows you to other devices, and is shown under the `Notifications` row in Settings. Single chats can override it.

## Set notifications for one group

Phone: on the Workspaces tab, press and hold the group and tap `Group notifications`. Pick `All group activity` (everything that happens there), `Group posts, mentions, and replies`, `Mentions and replies` (only when someone mentions you or replies to your posts) or `Nothing`. The current level has a checkmark.
Desktop: hover the group in the sidebar, open its three-dot menu and pick `Group notifications`. `Back` returns to the menu.
Notes: the same choices open as a full screen headed `Notifications` from `Group info & settings`, then `Notifications`. Under `Group notifications` the menu shows the current level in short form, for example "Mentions and replies (custom)" if you set it for this group, or "All posts (app default)" if it follows the app-wide setting.

## Set notifications for one channel

Phone: open the group, press and hold the channel in its channel list and tap `Channel notifications`. Pick `All channel activity`, `Channel posts, mentions, and replies`, `Mentions and replies` or `Nothing`. Another way in: open the channel, tap its name at the top, then tap `Notifications`.
Desktop: hover the channel in the sidebar, open its three-dot menu and pick `Channel notifications`.
Notes: on your node, a channel with no setting of its own follows its group's level, and a group with none follows the app-wide one. The menu doesn't show that: for an unset channel, the checkmark and the "(app default)" label show the app-wide level, even when its group has its own. In a group with a single channel, tapping the name opens the group's details, so `Notifications` there sets the group's level.

## Set notifications for a DM or group chat

Phone: on the Workspaces tab, press and hold the DM and tap `Chat notifications`, then pick `All messages` or `Nothing`.
Desktop: hover the DM in the sidebar, open its three-dot menu and pick `Chat notifications`.
Notes: DMs and group chats are all or nothing; there is no mentions-only level for them. The setting is only reachable from the list. Tapping a DM's name at the top of the chat opens the person's profile, not its notification settings.

## Mute a chat completely

Phone: on the Workspaces tab, swipe the chat to the left and tap the crossed-out bell. To unmute, swipe left again and tap the bell. For full silence from a group, set its notifications to `Nothing` instead.
Desktop: there is no swipe. Open the chat's three-dot menu, pick its notifications row, then `Nothing`.
Notes: muting a DM by swipe sets it to `Nothing`. Muting a group by swipe sets it to `Mentions and replies`, so mentions still reach you. Channels inside a group can't be swiped; use their notifications menu. A muted chat keeps counting unread messages and shows a small crossed-out bell beside the count. Unmuting by swipe puts the chat back on the default.

## Go back to the default for a chat

Phone: on the Settings tab, tap `Notifications` and scroll to `Overrides`. It lists every group, channel and DM that has its own level. Tap the X beside one to remove its own level, so it goes back to the default.
Desktop: click the gear in the left rail, then `Notifications`; the same `Overrides` list is at the bottom.
Notes: `Overrides` only appears once at least one chat has its own level. A chat's own notification menu has no "use default" choice, so this list is the way back. The one shortcut is a chat you muted by swiping on the phone: unmuting it the same way also returns it to the default. Muted threads are not listed here.

## Mute a thread

Phone: press and hold a reply in the thread, or the message the thread hangs off, and tap `Mute thread`. `Unmute thread` in the same menu undoes it.
Desktop: hover the message and use its three-dot menu.
Notes: the option only appears on replies and on messages that already have replies, and only while the app is connected.

## Turn on push notifications on my phone

Phone: during setup the app shows a `Notifications` screen. Tap `Next`, then allow notifications on the phone's own prompt. If you haven't answered that prompt yet, the app asks again when it opens. Once you've refused and the phone will no longer ask, a card titled `Stay in the loop` appears at the bottom of the Workspaces tab. Tap `Settings` on it to open the phone's settings for the app and switch notifications on there.
Notes: `Not now` hides the card. It comes back a few times, about half an hour apart, then stops; you can still open the phone's settings yourself. The app has no on/off switch for push. To stop it from inside the app, set the app-wide level to `Nothing`.

## What a push notification shows and where tapping it goes

Phone: a push for a message shows who sent it and the message text. In a group it is titled with the group and channel, or only the group if it has one channel. Tapping it opens that chat, in the thread if it was a reply. A request to join opens the group's member list. A group invite opens the Workspaces tab with the invite showing. A new DM opens the DM.
Notes: you get no banner for the chat you have open at that moment. Notifications from one chat are grouped together, and reading the chat in the app removes them. On Android, message notifications have a `Mark as read` button; iPhone notifications have no buttons.

## The number on the app icon

Phone: on iPhone the app icon shows a count of things you were notified about and haven't read. It drops to zero once you've read them all, or straight away with `Mark all as read` on the Activity tab.
Desktop: the desktop app's dock icon counts the notifications that arrived while its window wasn't in front, and resets when you come back to it.
Notes: on Android the app doesn't set its own count on the icon. In a browser there is no unread count in the tab title.

## Get notifications in a browser

Desktop: in the browser, click the gear in the left rail, then `Notifications`. Under `Browser notifications`, click `Enable` and allow it in the browser's prompt. The line under the heading then reads `Enabled`.
Notes: `Not enabled` means the browser hasn't asked you yet. `Blocked in browser` means you refused before; the `Enable` button is gone and you have to allow notifications for the site in the browser's own settings. Notifications follow your notification levels, only appear while Tlon Messenger isn't the window you're looking at, and need a tab with it left open. Clicking one opens the chat. The section is missing in the phone app, the desktop app, and on a page not loaded over https.

## Notifications in the desktop app

Desktop: there is nothing to switch on in the app. While its window isn't in front, the desktop app shows a system notification with a sound for messages your notification levels let through.
Notes: clicking one brings the window forward and marks that chat as read, but doesn't jump to the chat. To quieten it, lower your levels under `Notifications` in Settings, or use the computer's own notification settings for the app. The `Browser notifications` section only appears in a browser, not here.

## Notifications when a contact joins

Phone: when someone from your phone's synced contacts turns up on Tlon Messenger, you get a notification with their name followed by "is on Tlon" and the line `Tap to say hi`. Tapping it opens their profile. When several are found at once, it says how many and `Tap to see who`, and tapping opens your Contacts.
Who: people who have synced their phone contacts and allowed notifications for the app.
Notes: there is no switch for these in the app, and the notification levels don't affect them. They stop only if you turn notifications off for the app in the phone's settings. They don't appear on desktop.

## Get told when my node has woken up

Phone: if your node has been asleep, the app shows a waiting screen when you open it. When notifications aren't allowed yet, a sheet pops up with a `Notify me when it's ready` button; tap it and allow notifications. If you refused them earlier, the button reads `View notification settings` and opens the phone's settings.
Who: hosted accounts.
Notes: you can close the app while you wait. With notifications already allowed, the reminder is set without asking. It is a notification titled `Your node is now online`, timed for about ten minutes later rather than sent at the exact moment the node is up.

## Why am I not getting notifications

Phone: check four things. On the Settings tab, `Notifications` shouldn't be set to `Nothing`. On the same screen, look under `Overrides` for the chat in question; it may have its own lower level. On the Workspaces tab, a crossed-out bell on the chat's row means it is muted. And notifications must be allowed for the app in the phone's settings; a `Stay in the loop` card on the Workspaces tab means they aren't.
Desktop: in a browser, also check that `Browser notifications` reads `Enabled`.
Notes: you never get a banner for the chat you're looking at, and at `DMs, mentions, and replies only` ordinary group messages don't notify.

## Change the notification sound, or set quiet hours

Notes: Tlon Messenger doesn't have these. There is no sound picker, no schedule for silencing notifications at set times, no way to mute a chat for a set period, and no notifications by email. Use the phone's or computer's own focus and sound settings, or lower a level under `Notifications` in Settings and raise it again later. Notifications that arrive while the phone app is open show a banner without a sound.
