# Groups

Creating, joining, leaving and running a group, which the phone app calls a workspace: group types and templates, invite links and join requests, the group info and settings screen, privacy, members, roles, inviting people, leaving and deleting.

## Make a new workspace with my Tlonbot in it

Phone: on the Workspaces tab, tap the plus icon at the top right, then `New Workspace`. The workspace is made straight away and its chat opens.
Desktop: click the plus icon at the top of the `Home` sidebar. In the `Start a conversation` box, pick `New Workspace`.
Who: accounts with a hosted Tlonbot. Without one, `New Workspace` is not in the menu.
Notes: there is nothing to choose: no type, name or people. It is a group with your bot in it and a `General` chat, named `My agent group` until someone renames it. Invite other people afterwards.

## Create a group without my bot

Phone: on the Workspaces tab, tap the plus icon at the top right, then `New group`. On `Create a group`, pick `Quick group`, `Basic group` or a template. `Basic group` asks for a name; the others skip that. Pick people, or nobody, and tap `Create group`.
Desktop: click the plus icon at the top of the `Home` sidebar and pick `New group`. The same steps follow in dialogs.
Who: on the phone, `New group` only shows on accounts without a hosted Tlonbot; accounts with one get `New Workspace` instead. Desktop always shows `New group`.
Notes: new groups start out secret. On the phone, a group with one channel opens straight into its chat, and one with several opens on its channel list.

## Pick a group type or template

Phone: the `Create a group` sheet offers three kinds. `Quick group` is `Start chatting right away with default settings`: one chat called `General`, and no name asked. `Basic group` is `Group with chat, gallery, and notebook channels`, and you name it. Below those is a template card; swipe it sideways to see the others.
Desktop: click the arrows on either side of the template card.
Notes: the templates are `Book Club`, `Cooking Club`, `Music`, `Running Club`, `Cinema Club` and `Garden Club`. Each makes a chat, a gallery and a notebook named for the theme, for example `Book chat`, `Now reading` and `Reviews`, and the group takes the template's name. Channels can be added or removed afterwards.

## Name a group while creating it

Phone: only `Basic group` asks for a name. After you pick it, `Name your group` opens. Type in the `Group name` box and tap `Next`. `Next` stays greyed out until you type something.
Notes: a `Quick group` gets no name, so it shows its members' names, or `Untitled group` while you're the only one in it. A template group takes the template's name. An admin can rename any of them later with `Rename` on `Group info & settings`.

## Add people while creating a group

Phone: the last step of `New group` is headed `Select contacts to invite`. Your contacts are listed. Tap each person you want, and tap again to unselect. To find someone, type in `Filter by nickname or id`; typing a person's full ID finds them even when they aren't a contact. Then tap `Create group`.
Notes: `Create group` works with nobody selected, so you can make the group first and invite people later. A contact marked `App version mismatch` can't be picked. `New Workspace` has no people step.

## Join a group with a code

Phone: on the Workspaces tab, tap the plus icon at the top right, then `Join a group with a code (reference)` at the bottom of the menu. Type or paste the code into `Enter group code` and tap `Go`. The group's preview appears, with a button to join or ask for an invite.
Desktop: the same link is at the bottom of the `Start a conversation` box.
Who: on the phone the link only shows on accounts without a hosted Tlonbot. Desktop always has it.
Notes: the code is the group's ID, like "~host/group-name", or a group reference containing it. A member gets the ID from `Copy group ID` on the group's info screen. If the code isn't a valid ID, `Go` just closes the sheet. `Group not found` means nothing matched.

## Open a group invite link

Phone: tap the link while you're signed in. Tlon Messenger opens on the Workspaces tab with that group's preview sheet. Tap the button it offers: `Accept invite`, or `Join group` for a public group. If you're already a member it says `Go to group`.
Notes: the app redeems the link for your account in the background, so the sheet can take a moment to appear. Which button you get depends on the group: a private group you hold no invite for shows `Request invite`. If the sheet doesn't appear, look for the group in your list on the Workspaces tab and tap it there.

## See a group's details before joining

Phone: the preview sheet shows the group's icon, name and host, a badge reading `Public Group`, `Private Group` or `Secret Group`, whether the host's node is `Online` or `Offline`, and the `Description` if one is set. Under that is the action: `Join group`, `Request invite`, `Accept invite` with `Reject invite`, or `Go to group` if you're already in.
Notes: the preview opens when you tap a group that's waiting in your list, a group reference someone posted in a chat, or a group invite link or notification. An `Incompatible` badge means the group's host runs an incompatible version of the app; joining is blocked until one of you updates.

## Join a public group

Phone: open the group's preview from a link, a reference in a chat or a code, and tap `Join group`. The button changes to `Joining, please wait...`. Once you're in, the sheet closes and the group is in your list with a `NEW` badge.
Notes: `Join group` only shows for public groups. A private group shows `Request invite`, and a secret group needs an invite.

## Accept or decline a group invite

Phone: the invite shows in your list on the Workspaces tab with an `Invite` badge. Tap it, then tap `Accept invite` to join or `Reject invite` to decline.
Desktop: the invite is in the `Home` sidebar. Click it for the same preview.
Notes: after accepting you see `Joining, please wait...`; the sheet closes when you're in. Rejecting removes the invite from your list. Tapping a group-invite notification opens the same sheet. If the invite hasn't reached your node yet, the sheet shows a spinner and closes by itself after about 15 seconds; open the invite from the list once it appears. Invites in the list have no press-and-hold menu.

## Ask to join a private group, or cancel the request

Phone: open the group's preview and tap `Request invite`. To take it back, tap the group in your list on the Workspaces tab, where it now has a `Requested` badge, and tap `Cancel request`.
Notes: while you wait, the preview says `Invite requested`. An admin of the group has to accept the request. Only private groups take requests. A secret group's preview says `This group is secret` and `You need an invite to join this group`, with nothing to tap.

## Fix a group that is stuck joining or failed to join

Phone: on the Workspaces tab the group has a `Joining` or `Errored` badge. Tap it to open its preview, then tap `Cancel join`. That clears the attempt so you can start again from the invite, link or code.
Notes: while joining, the preview says `Joining, please wait...`. After a failure it says `Joining failed` with `Something went wrong while trying to join this group.` If instead it says `The group host is running an incompatible version of the app.`, trying again won't help until you or the host updates.

## Open a group's info and settings

Phone: on the Workspaces tab, press and hold the group and tap `Group info & settings`. Or open the group and tap its name at the top.
Desktop: hover the group in the sidebar and open its three-dot menu, or right-click the group, then pick `Group info & settings`. Clicking the group's name above its channel list also works.
Notes: in a group with several channels, the name at the top of an open channel leads to that channel's info instead. Go back to the group's channel list and tap the group's name there.

## What's on the Group info & settings screen

Phone: every member sees, from the top: the group's icon and name with a line like "Secret group with 4 members"; a row of buttons (`Invite` if you're allowed to invite, `Mark read` when something is unread, `Pin` or `Unpin`, `Forward reference`, `Copy group ID`); the `Description` if one is set; the connection to the group's host; `Notifications`; the first five members; and `Leave group`.
Who: admins also get `Rename` at the top right, the rows `Privacy`, `Roles` and `Channels`, and `Manage members` under the member list. The group's host gets `Delete group` in place of `Leave group`.
Notes: `Privacy`, `Roles` and `Channels` each show their current value or count and open their own screen.

## Find out why a group's settings are greyed out

Phone: open `Group info & settings` and read the first row of the settings block. It shows the connection to the group's host: `Connected`, `Connecting...` or `Disconnected`.
Who: only admins see `Rename`, `Privacy`, `Roles` and `Channels` at all.
Notes: those controls only work while the host's node can be reached. Until the row says `Connected` they are greyed out, `Save` on the edit screen is disabled, and admins of a private or secret group lose the `Invite` button on this screen. If you host the group yourself it always counts as connected. If it stays `Disconnected`, the host's node is offline or unreachable, so try again later.

## Share a group in a chat, or copy its ID

Phone: open `Group info & settings`. To post the group into a chat, tap `Forward reference`, pick the chat on the `Forward group` sheet, then tap the button at the bottom, which reads "Forward to" and the chat's name. To copy the group's ID instead, tap `Copy group ID`; you'll see `Copied!`.
Notes: people in that chat can tap the reference to open the group's preview. The ID is what `Join a group with a code (reference)` asks for. Neither one gets around privacy: a private group still needs an admin to accept, and a secret group still needs an invite.

## Rename a group, or change its icon or description

Phone: open `Group info & settings` and tap `Rename` at the top right. On `Edit group info`, change the `Name`, tap `Change icon image` to choose a picture, or edit the `Description`. Tap `Save`.
Who: admins, while the group's host can be reached.
Notes: `Rename` is the only way in, and it covers the icon and description too. The name can be up to 30 characters and the description up to 300. To remove an icon, tap the icon button and choose `Clear`. If the button says `Storage not configured`, the node has no storage set up for uploads. Groups have no cover image setting.

## Change a group's privacy

Phone: open `Group info & settings` and tap `Privacy`. On the `Group privacy` screen tap `Public`, `Private` or `Secret`. The change applies as soon as you tap; there is no save button.
Who: admins, while the group's host can be reached.
Notes: the `Privacy` row shows the current setting. New groups start out secret.

## Understand public, private and secret groups

Notes: the app's own wording on the `Group privacy` screen, and what follows from each:

- `Public`: `Anyone can join this group without needing an invite.` Someone who finds the group gets a `Join group` button, and any member can invite people.
- `Private`: `Users must be invited to join this group.` Someone who finds it can tap `Request invite` and wait for an admin to accept. Only admins can invite.
- `Secret`: `Membership is invite only and private. Only administrators can invite new members.` Someone without an invite sees `This group is secret` and can't ask to join.

## See who's in a group, or find a member

Phone: open `Group info & settings`. The member block lists the first five people. Tap `See all` (admins see `Manage members`) to open `Members`, and type in `Search by name or ID` to find someone. Tap a person to open their card; tap the top of the card for their full profile.
Notes: people are grouped under each role's name, then `Invited` for invites not yet accepted, then `Members` for everyone without a role. Admins also see `Join Requests` and `Banned Users`. The `See all` row only appears when the group has more than five members or you're allowed to invite; in a smaller group everyone is already listed. Another person's card always has `Block`.

## Give a member a role, or make them an admin

Phone: open `Group info & settings`, tap `Manage members`, tap the person, then `Assign role`. Tap a role to give it to them. Roles they already hold have a checkmark; tap one of those to take it away.
Who: admins.
Notes: to make someone an admin, give them the `Admin` role the same way. The group's host can't have their admin role removed. People who have only been invited can't be given a role until they join. To change a role's whole member list at once, use the `Roles` screen instead.

## Remove someone from a group

Phone: open `Group info & settings`, tap `Manage members`, tap the person, then `Kick User`. Confirm with `Kick User`, or tap `Cancel`.
Desktop: the confirmation is a plain browser dialog.
Who: admins. You can't kick yourself.
Notes: the confirmation says `This user will be removed from the group.` and warns that kicking them cancels every invitation they've sent. Kicking only removes them. To keep someone out, use `Ban User` instead.

## Ban or unban someone

Phone: to ban, open `Group info & settings`, tap `Manage members`, tap the person, then `Ban User`. To unban, find them under `Banned Users` on the same screen, tap them, then `Unban User`.
Who: admins. The group's host, other admins and you yourself can't be banned.
Notes: there is no confirmation; the ban happens on the tap. Banning removes the person from the group and lists them under `Banned Users`. Unbanning only lifts the ban. It doesn't put them back in the group, so invite them again if you want them back.

## Approve or reject a request to join

Phone: when someone asks to join, a `New join requests` notice shows in the group; tap `Review`. Or open `Group info & settings` and tap `Manage members`. Under `Join Requests`, tap the person, then `Accept request` or `Reject request`.
Who: admins. Only private groups get requests.
Notes: the `Join request` sheet also has a row that opens the person's profile, so you can check who they are first. Dismissing the notice doesn't answer anything; requests stay under `Join Requests` until you accept or reject them.

## Cancel an invite that hasn't been accepted

Phone: open `Group info & settings`, tap `Manage members`, find the person under `Invited`, tap them, then `Revoke Invite`.
Who: admins.
Notes: there is no confirmation. `Invited` lists people who were invited by name and haven't joined yet. Revoking takes them off that list.

## See a group's roles, or create one

Phone: open `Group info & settings` and tap `Roles`. `Group Roles` lists each role with the number of members who hold it. To make one, tap `New` at the top right. On `Add role`, fill in `Title`, add a `Description` if you like, tap `Members` to choose who gets it, then tap `Save`.
Who: admins, while the group's host can be reached.
Notes: `Title` is required. A role is only a title, a description and a list of members; what it unlocks is set channel by channel, in each channel's permissions. The last row, `Members`, stands for `All group members` and can't be opened.

## Rename or edit a role

Phone: open `Group info & settings`, tap `Roles`, then tap the role. Change the `Title` or `Description`, or tap `Members` to change who holds it, then tap `Save`.
Who: admins.
Notes: the screen is headed "Edit" and the role's name. If any channels use the role, they are listed at the top. Going back with unsaved changes asks `Discard changes?`; tap `Discard` to drop them. The `Admin` role's title and description can't be edited.

## Choose which members have a role

Phone: open `Group info & settings`, tap `Roles`, tap the role, then tap `Members`. On the `Select members` screen, tap people to tick or untick them; `Search by name or ID` narrows the list. Tap the back arrow at the top, then tap `Save` on the role.
Who: admins.
Notes: each person's current roles show under their name, or `No roles`. Nothing changes until you tap `Save` on the role screen. People who have been invited but haven't joined aren't listed.

## Delete a role

Phone: open `Group info & settings`, tap `Roles`, tap the role, then tap `Delete role`.
Who: admins.
Notes: there is no confirmation. `Delete role` is greyed out while any member still holds the role or any channel uses it, and the screen says `This role cannot be deleted, it is still in use for some users or channels.` First take everyone off it (tap `Members`, untick them all, go back, `Save`) and remove it from the channels listed at the top of the screen, then open the role again. The `Admin` role has no delete button.

## Understand the Admin role

Notes: `Admin` is the built-in role on the `Group Roles` screen. It can't be renamed or deleted. In the app, admins are the people who see `Rename`, `Privacy`, `Roles` and `Channels` on the group's info screen. They can assign roles, kick and ban members, answer join requests, revoke invites, and invite people to a private or secret group. An admin who isn't the group's host still can't delete the group; only the host sees `Delete group`. The host's own admin role can't be taken away.

## Invite people who already use Tlon Messenger to a group

Phone: on the Workspaces tab, press and hold the group and tap `Invite people`. On the `Invite People` screen, tap the people you want, or type in `Filter by nickname, @p` to find someone. The button at the bottom changes from `Select people to invite` to "Invite 2 and continue", with your own count; tap it.
Desktop: hover the group in the sidebar, open its three-dot menu and pick `Invite people`. The picker opens as a panel over the page, not a full screen.
Who: any member of a public group. In a private or secret group, only admins.
Notes: `Invite` and `Invite People` on `Group info & settings` open the same picker. A contact marked `App version mismatch` can't be picked.

## Share a link that invites people to a group

Phone: on the Workspaces tab, press and hold the group and tap `Invite people`. The link is at the top. Tap `Share link` to send it through another app, or `Copy` to copy it.
Desktop: `Share link` uses the browser's own share sheet where there is one, and otherwise copies the link.
Who: any member of a public group. In a private or secret group, only admins.
Notes: while the link is being made, the box says `Generating invite link...` and both buttons wait. `Invite links are disabled` or `Error generating invite link` mean there is no link to share right now; invite people by name from the list underneath instead.

## Find out why I can't invite people to a group

Notes: in a private or secret group, only admins can invite. Everyone else sees `Invites disabled` with `Only admins may invite people to this group.` in the group's press-and-hold menu, and no `Invite` button on `Group info & settings`. In a public group every member can invite. If you are an admin and `Invite` is missing from the info screen, the group's host can't be reached right now; the press-and-hold menu still has `Invite people`. To let every member invite, an admin can set the group to `Public` under `Privacy`.

## Leave a group

Phone: open `Group info & settings`, scroll to the bottom and tap `Leave group`. Confirm with `Leave Group`, or tap `Cancel`.
Desktop: the confirmation is a plain browser dialog.
Who: every member except the group's host. The host has no leave option, only `Delete group`.
Notes: the confirmation says `You will no longer receive updates from this group.` and warns that leaving cancels any invitations you've sent. The group then drops out of your list. Leaving is only on this screen; the group's press-and-hold menu doesn't have it.

## Delete a group

Phone: open `Group info & settings`, scroll to the bottom and tap `Delete group`. A box asks "Delete" and the group's name, with `This action cannot be undone.` Tap `Delete group` to confirm, or `Cancel`.
Who: only the group's host, the person whose node the group was made on. Other admins don't see `Delete group`.
Notes: the host can't leave their own group, so deleting is the host's only way out of it.

## Hand a group over to a different owner

Notes: Tlon Messenger has no way to move a group to another host or owner. The host is the node the group was made on, and that never changes. What you can do is give other people the `Admin` role, which lets them run the group day to day. Only the host can delete it, and the host can't leave it.
