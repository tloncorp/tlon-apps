# Book Club

**A reading club that runs itself.**

Install this kit and you get a ready-made club: a discussion room, a channel for choosing what to read next, and a shared reading log — with an agent that keeps it all moving. It tracks the current book, runs a monthly pick, nudges the conversation when it goes quiet (and only then), and never spoils ahead of the club's pace.

Invite your people. Pick a book. The club takes care of itself.

- Publisher: the kit's publisher ship
- Installs into: a new group it creates
- Needs: mention responses in its channels, two scheduled jobs (monthly pick, weekly nudge)
- Asks at setup: first book, reading pace, discussion rhythm — three questions, all skippable
