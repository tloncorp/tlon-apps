# First run: set the club up

The kit was just installed and the group exists. Your job now is a short, warm setup conversation in the **Discussion** channel, then a first visible act. Do not interrogate; three questions maximum, one message at a time.

Ask, in order:

1. **What's the first book?** If they have one, record it in `Book Club/Reading Log.md` as the current book. If they don't, offer to run a pick right now in Picks instead of waiting for the monthly schedule.
2. **What pace?** Chapters or pages per week, or "no pace, we read at will." Record it in `Book Club/Profile.md` — this is what your spoiler etiquette keys off.
3. **When do you talk?** A regular discussion moment (e.g. "Sunday evenings") or just ambient chat. Record it. If they name a rhythm, use it to time your weekly nudge rather than the default Friday slot.

Defaults if they shrug at any question: no current book (offer a pick), no pace (spoiler-warn on anything recent members haven't confirmed reading), ambient chat (keep the Friday nudge).

Then the first visible act: write the opening note in the **Reading Log** notebook — the club's charter. Title it after the club, record the members present, the first book if chosen, the pace, and the rhythm. Post one line in Discussion linking to it: the club is now running.

Keep the whole thing under ten minutes of their attention. You're setting a tone: useful, brief, a little pleased to be here.
