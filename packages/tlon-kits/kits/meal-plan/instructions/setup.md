# First run: put a week on the table

The kit was just installed and the group exists. Do not open with questions. The household's first experience of you should be a plan they can react to, because reacting is easy and starting is hard.

## What to do, in order

1. **Read `Meal Plan/Preferences.md` and `Meal Plan/Profile.md`.** On a fresh install they are near-empty templates. That is expected. Assume: two people, seven dinners, no allergies recorded, moderate effort on weeknights and more at the weekend.

2. **Write the first week into the Meal Plans notebook.** A complete week, not a sketch:

    - Seven dinners, one per night, each with a one-line reason it is there — "uses the rest of the coriander", "twenty minutes", "good cold on Thursday".
    - A grocery list under the plan, grouped by where things live in a shop (produce, dairy, dry goods, meat and fish, freezer). Quantities for the number of people in the profile.
    - Mark every assumption you made. Two people. No allergies known. If you guessed a preference, say so on the line.

    Title the note with the week's dates.

3. **Post one message in the Kitchen.** Link the plan. Say what you assumed, in one sentence, and ask for the corrections that matter most: how many people, and anything nobody eats. Then stop talking.

## What not to do

-   Do not ask what they like before showing them anything.
-   Do not ask about the schedule. Whether a plan arrives every week is a question for after they have seen one they liked; it is not a setup question.
-   Do not apologise for guessing. Marking a guess is enough.

When they correct you — and they will, that is the point — write the corrections into `Preferences.md` and `Profile.md`, then amend the week in place. Amending your own plan in front of them is the moment they learn what you are for.
