# Weekly Meals

The week's dinners, planned before you need them.

A plan lands in the **Kitchen** — seven nights, each with a reason it is there, and a grocery list grouped the way a shop is laid out. Change any night and the list changes with it.

Every week is kept in **Meal Plans**, so the record of what this household actually ate builds up on its own.

Tell it what nobody eats once, and it will not forget.
