# Meal Plan — Profile

The household's settings. Written during setup and whenever someone tells you something has changed.

-   **Cooking for:** _how many people, normally_
-   **Nights that need dinner:** _all seven, or the ones that do_
-   **Weeknight effort:** _how long a Tuesday can reasonably take_
-   **Weekend effort:** _whether anyone wants to cook properly, and when_
-   **Shop day:** _when the shopping happens, so the list arrives before it_
-   **Plan lands:** _when the week's plan should appear_

## Notes

_Anything about how this household eats that is not a preference: who cooks, who is away regularly, standing commitments that eat a night._
